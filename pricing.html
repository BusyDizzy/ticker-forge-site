Pricing ​

TickerForge pricing is built around research workflows: stock checks, Smart Alerts, portfolio diagnostics, portfolio stress testing, market context, and screeners. If you are comparing a trading platform with portfolio stress testing pricing, note the distinction: TickerForge is not a broker or execution platform. It is educational decision-support software that helps you review risk, concentration, alerts, and research evidence alongside the broker you already use.

Paid access is currently activated through Telegram Stars. USD prices are shown only as a reference while website card payments are being prepared.

Decision-support software, not financial advice.

TickerForge provides software-based stock research, portfolio diagnostics, market context, and decision-support workflows for educational and informational purposes. It does not provide personalized financial advice, brokerage services, trade execution, portfolio management, or guaranteed investment outcomes.

What paid access unlocks

Paid plans unlock software workflows such as expanded alerts, portfolio tracking, screeners, reports, and workspace features. They do not buy performance or investment outcomes.

What this does not include: Plans do not include personalized financial advice, brokerage services, trade execution, account management, tax advice, or guaranteed investment results.

Portfolio Stress Testing Pricing ​

Portfolio analytics are included in TickerForge plans as research and decision-support tools. Depending on the plan, users can review stock checks, portfolio diagnostics, portfolio health, concentration, sector exposure, cash buffer, stress-test style drawdown sensitivity, market context, and alerts from the same workflow.

Start with Free if you want a browser-based portfolio snapshot and health check. Standard adds recurring portfolio monitoring and alerts. Pro is designed for deeper portfolio analytics, advanced research views, and export-ready workflows where available.

TickerForge is not a brokerage, does not execute trades, and does not manage portfolios. Pricing covers analytics software for research workflows, not portfolio management or personalized financial advice.

Portfolio Pricing FAQ ​

Does TickerForge include portfolio stress testing? Yes. Portfolio diagnostics include stress-test style reports that estimate downside and upside sensitivity from holdings, cash, exposure, and beta assumptions.

Which plan includes portfolio diagnostics? Free includes browser diagnostics and demos. Standard adds weekly tracking and alerts. Pro includes advanced portfolio analytics and exports where available.

Is TickerForge a brokerage? No. TickerForge does not hold assets, connect to brokerage custody, or execute trades.

Is this financial advice? No. TickerForge provides educational decision-support software and research diagnostics, not personalized investment advice.

Can I use TickerForge before changing my portfolio? Yes. You can import holdings to review portfolio health, concentration, sector exposure, cash buffer, and stress-test impact before making decisions elsewhere.

Delivery: Email And Telegram ​

TickerForge can send follow-up research by email, Telegram, or both.

  • Email is useful for stock snapshots, portfolio snapshot PDFs, alert summaries, and payment waitlist updates.
  • Telegram is required for Stars payment, account activation, bot menus, portfolio creation/import, and Telegram alerts.

If you start from a website widget, the Free path asks for email or Telegram after the stock or portfolio result. If you choose Standard or Pro access, the widget sends you to this pricing page so you can choose the current payment option without repeating the same delivery step twice.

Widget Flow Logic ​

Stock Check: Free continues to email or Telegram and can enable one Smart Alert, up to the free allowance of 5 tracked stocks. When the allowance is exhausted, upgrade prompts should point back to this pricing page or the bot upgrade flow.

Portfolio Diagnostics: Free sends the portfolio snapshot and trial context by email. To create/import the portfolio and enable tracking, continue in Telegram. The free trial should count tracking runs for the portfolio instead of resetting just because a new portfolio snapshot was created.

Standard / Pro Interest: Choosing paid access from a widget records the product context and opens pricing. It should not ask the same email/Telegram question again inside the widget.
