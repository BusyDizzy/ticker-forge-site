TickerForge Ambassador Program ​

The Ambassador Program is for people who already talk about research methods, portfolio risk, and how they check a company before acting — analysts, finance creators, community moderators, and long-term users who recommend the tools they actually use.

You share a personal referral link. When someone who arrived through it activates a paid plan, you receive 20% of that subscription every month for as long as they stay subscribed. There is no cap on the number of referrals or on monthly rewards.

Decision-support software, not financial advice.

TickerForge provides software-based stock research, portfolio diagnostics, market context, and decision-support workflows for educational and informational purposes. It does not provide personalized financial advice, brokerage services, trade execution, portfolio management, or guaranteed investment outcomes.

How It Works ​

  1. Get your referral link. Open the TickerForge bot in Telegram, go to Ambassador Program, and copy your personal link.
  2. Share it where it belongs. Posts, newsletters, community threads, comparison content — anywhere your audience is already discussing research and portfolio decisions.
  3. Earn monthly. Every referred user on a paid plan generates a recurring reward while their subscription stays active.

Attribution is first-touch and permanent: once a user is bound to your link, they stay attributed to you. A user who already belongs to another ambassador is never re-attributed.

Reward Structure ​

Rewards are 20% of the referred user's subscription, recalculated every month against their current plan. Plans are charged in Telegram Stars; USD figures are references from the pricing page, not a separate price list.

Referred user's planPlan priceYour recurring reward
Early Access700 Stars / month (price-locked 12 months)140 Stars / month (~$2.20 reference)
Standard1,200 Stars / month (~$19 reference)240 Stars / month (~$3.80 reference)
Pro2,000 Stars / month (~$39 reference)400 Stars / month (~$7.80 reference)

In addition:

  • 1 free month of Standard for every 5 paying referrals;
  • no limit on the number of referrals;
  • no limit on total monthly rewards;
  • rewards stop when a referred subscription lapses and resume if it is reactivated.

Ambassador Levels ​

Your level is derived from the number of active paying referrals in the current month. The reward percentage is the same at every level — levels add access and recognition, not a higher rate.

LevelActive paying referralsWhat it adds
Bronze1–420% recurring rewards, Bronze badge in your profile
Silver5–19+1 free month of Standard per 5 referrals, priority support
Gold20–49Monthly insights report (macro, sectors, signals context)
Platinum50–99Extended insights, "TickerForge Advisor" badge, early access to new features
Elite100+All Platinum benefits, monthly personal portfolio analysis, partner spotlight, direct line to the founder

Each level includes everything from the levels below it. Check your current level and referral count with Ambassador Levels in the bot.

Payouts ​

  • Rewards are calculated per calendar month from the paid plans held by your referred users during that month.
  • The amount shown in My Monthly Earnings in the bot is an estimate based on current subscription state, not a settled invoice.
  • Payout details are confirmed individually once you have your first paying referrals. Ask through the bot or contact if you need the terms before you start.

What We Ask ​

The program is open, but the positioning has to hold. When you promote TickerForge:

  • present it as research and decision-support software, not as advice, signals, or a way to make money on the markets;
  • disclose that your link is affiliate-supported, per the rules of the platform you post on;
  • do not promise investment returns, guaranteed outcomes, or specific entry and exit points on TickerForge's behalf;
  • do not use paid search or ads on TickerForge brand terms without written agreement;
  • keep it honest: describe what the product actually does — stock checks, portfolio diagnostics, concentration and exposure review, market context, alerts.

Referrals from spam, incentivized sign-ups, self-referral, or fake accounts do not count and can end participation.

What This Program Is Not ​

The Ambassador Program is a referral arrangement for software subscriptions. It is not:

  • revenue sharing on investment results;
  • a promise that referred users will subscribe or stay subscribed;
  • an advisory, brokerage, or introducing-broker relationship;
  • a licence to present yourself as a TickerForge employee or representative.

TickerForge is educational decision-support software. It does not provide personalized investment advice, execute trades, or guarantee outcomes — and neither does anything you say about it on our behalf.

Getting Started ​

  1. Open TickerForge in Telegram.
  2. Go to Ambassador Program.
  3. Copy your personal referral link.
  4. Share it where your audience already discusses research and portfolio decisions.

If you run a channel, newsletter, or community and want a structured collaboration rather than a personal link, see the Partner Program — a partner pilot and the Ambassador Program run in parallel, and the same audience can arrive through a tracked pilot link and subscribe through your referral link.
