What You Can Inspect ​

Choose a model, then start with Portfolio View to understand what it owns, how it is allocated, and how its virtual balance is performing. This creates a logical path from portfolio construction to portfolio diagnosis.

When you are ready, switch to Analyze. It opens Portfolio Snapshot first, giving you a concise summary of portfolio health, cash buffer, position and sector breadth, concentration, and an estimated downside scenario.

The four diagnostic views answer different questions:

  • Portfolio Snapshot shows the most important scores and structural exposures on one screen.
  • Health Check explains strengths, concentration warnings, risk factors, and practical areas to review.
  • Sector Allocation reveals whether diversification is real or whether several holdings depend on the same industry cycle.
  • Stress Test estimates how the portfolio may react to downside and upside market shocks using the same scenario engine used by the Telegram reports.

The widget is read-only: it demonstrates the portfolio and its diagnostics without creating a portfolio in your account or placing trades. To analyze your holdings, open Import Portfolio. To create a new virtual portfolio using the same construction workflow, open the Portfolio Builder.

Portfolio Story, Risk, and Ownership ​

Portfolio Snapshot also combines stock-level decision data across the positions it can cover:

  • Story Heat estimates how strongly price leadership, trend persistence, valuation re-rating, growth, and sector participation are reinforcing the market narrative. A high score means the story is already visible in market behavior; it is not an instruction to trade.
  • Crowding Risk measures stretch, vertical price action, valuation pressure, weak confirmation, and market fragility. Higher values mean entry timing and position sizing deserve more caution.
  • Top Funds Regime weights each covered holding by its portfolio size, then shows whether tracked 13F funds are predominantly adding, selling, or sending mixed signals.
  • Insider Activity summarizes the latest open-market insider regime and the combined three-month purchases and sales across covered holdings.

Coverage is shown beside each block because not every security has complete decision, 13F, or Form 4 data. Missing holdings are excluded from the corresponding weighted score rather than treated as bearish signals.

Why Use a Model Portfolio? ​

A stock can look attractive by itself and still make a portfolio weaker. It may duplicate an existing sector exposure, increase market sensitivity, or become too large relative to the rest of the book. A model portfolio makes these relationships easier to see because every report is generated from the same set of positions.

For a deeper explanation of each report, visit Portfolio Analysis. The scoring logic is documented in the Portfolio Health Check methodology, while downside and margin scenarios are covered in the Portfolio Stress Test methodology.

Continue With Your Own Portfolio ​

The public model is an educational demonstration, not a recommendation or a promise of future performance. Your own portfolio may have different position sizes, cash needs, tax constraints, broker exposure, and risk tolerance.

Use the Stock Analysis Widget when you want to investigate an individual company before adding it. Then import your portfolio to run the same four diagnostic reports on your own holdings. Advanced access and portfolio features are described on the Pricing page.
