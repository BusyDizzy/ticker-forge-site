Daily Snapshot vs Weekly Regime ​

The Daily Snapshot is the faster-moving view. It helps you see current stress, risk appetite, macro pressure, and important divergences under the surface. It can change quickly and should not be interpreted as a standalone trading signal.

The Weekly Regime filters more of that short-term noise. Use it to understand whether the broader environment is supportive, defensive, recovering, trending, or becoming euphoric before you make portfolio-level decisions.

How to Use Both Views ​

Start with the weekly regime to establish the broader backdrop, then use the daily snapshot to judge whether current conditions confirm or challenge that backdrop. A supportive weekly trend with a temporarily defensive daily reading may call for patience rather than a complete change in thesis. When both views deteriorate together, investors may want to review concentration, position sizing, and exposure to fragile companies before adding more risk.

Do not treat a daily or weekly label as a mechanical buy or sell instruction. Look for confirmation across market stress, sector leadership, credit conditions, volatility, and the quality of the stocks you are considering. The widget is most useful as a context layer before a stock check or portfolio decision.
