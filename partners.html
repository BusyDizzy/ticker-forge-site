How Partner Cabinet works

🎁 Partner Benefits ​

Approved applicants start with 14 days of Standard Review Access, with no publications required. Any later Pilot has its own agreed access window. This applies beyond Telegram: a LinkedIn group owner, YouTube creator, Instagram finance blogger, newsletter author, or community operator can use the product to prepare better posts and route interested readers through tracked links.

  • ⭐ Standard access. 14 days of Standard Review Access with no publications required; then Standard for an agreed Pilot window.
  • 🧰 Partner Cabinet. Your own workspace inside the bot: pick a format, press one button, get a finished post with your link already in it. Post language, statistics and the monthly report live there too. How it works.
  • 🧠 Ready-to-post content. Not raw data to write around — a finished post, on live data, in the shape your format needs. Stock Verdict, Insider Activity, Fund Moves, weekly stock ideas, Market Regime and U.S. Economic Health, generated on demand.
  • 🔗 Tracked links. Every partner post carries your own link inside its call to action, and it sends readers straight into the matching TickerForge workflow.
  • 💰 Referral earnings. Up to 20% every month of what the people who came from you pay, for as long as they stay subscribed — the same rules as /ambassador. No cap on referrals or on monthly rewards.
  • 📊 Measurable results. Generated posts, bot starts per format, referrals, and estimated earnings in your Partner Cabinet.

What We Ask In Return ​

Review Access carries no publishing commitment. If you want to continue, we agree a Pilot first; regular collaboration is discussed only after that test.

  • Audience: from 1,000 real subscribers on the channel you post in, in a finance or investing topic. Smaller but strongly engaged communities are considered case by case.
  • Review Access: 14 days of Standard, no publications required. Pilot: 1–2 agreed test posts with research blocks and tracked links.
  • Qualified post: a real TickerForge check, your own voice, a clear educational disclaimer, and a tracked link. Only published posts we can verify count.
  • Disclosure: sponsored, affiliate-supported, or complimentary-access-supported content is disclosed by the partner.
  • Personal seat: access is approved case by case, time-limited, and non-transferable. Publishing commitments apply only after a Pilot or ongoing collaboration is agreed.

Full eligibility rules, commitments, and the exact conditions under which access ends are on a separate page, so this one stays readable.
