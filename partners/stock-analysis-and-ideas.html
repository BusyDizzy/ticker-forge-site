What This Is ​

A research layer for posts you already want to write. You keep your own voice and your own conclusion; TickerForge supplies the evidence block underneath it and a tracked link for readers who want to check the company themselves.

Every post carries a tracked link, so clicks, bot starts and referral attribution show up in the Partner Cabinet.

What the post is built on ​

A partner post is not a headline with a ticker in it. Every one is assembled from the same layer the product runs on, and that is the part a reader cannot get from a screenshot of a chart.

  • 60+ financial metrics per period, annual and quarterly. Revenue, margins, cash flow, debt, coverage, returns — collected per company and carried through every year and quarter we hold, so a change is measured rather than asserted.
  • Compressed into one screen, colour-coded. The point is not that the data exists; it is that a reader sees year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter movement, fundamental risk, and where growth changed direction without opening a filing.
  • Turnaround detection. Whether the quarterly numbers have started moving the other way after a stretch of decline — stated as a stage, not as an opinion.
  • A numeric TickerForge score, and its own 5-year trend. The score is compared against its own 5-year moving average, so a reader sees whether the company is rated better or worse than its own history, not only where it sits today.
  • Institutional ownership across 37 tracked funds. Not "a fund bought it": the statistics for that specific stock across a fixed, named set of 37 funds, so the answer is comparable between companies and between quarters.
  • Insider flow as a NET figure over 3 and 12 months. Not one eye-catching purchase — buying minus selling, over two windows, from people required to file every transaction.

That is what makes it fast to react to: the assembly work is already done, and what your audience sees is the conclusion with its evidence attached.

Stock Ideas ​

The rule chooses the company. An idea here is a filter result with its reasoning attached, not a name somebody liked — a fixed rule that a company either passes or does not, run over the same 60+ metrics, the 37-fund ownership layer and the insider NET windows above.

The formats ​

Available in Partner CabinetTurnaround

A company whose quarterly numbers have started moving the other way after a stretch of decline. Early evidence of recovery, not a recovery.

Available in Partner CabinetInsiders Are Buying

Meaningful net open-market insider buying over recent months, from people who file every transaction.

Available in Partner CabinetTriple Rated

One company clearing three fixed gates on the same day — the TickerForge score, the Piotroski F-Score and the Altman Z-Score. Says nothing about price.

Available in Partner CabinetWeekly Gems: Growth

Revenue, earnings and cash flow all still moving the right way on the trailing twelve months, with the business quality holding up underneath.

Available in Partner CabinetWeekly Gems: Value

Priced under a usable fair value estimate, where the business is not the reason for the discount.

Available in Partner CabinetWeekly Gems: Ownership

A good business reading where insiders, tracked funds, or both have been adding.

Available for partner pilotsAccumulation Watchlists

Themed watchlists — large caps, AI-era infrastructure, dividend compounders — built from the accumulation scan.

What makes it an idea rather than a tip ​

  • The rule is fixed and stated. Every format checks one specific thing and says which. A reader can disagree with the rule, which is more than they can do with a pick.
  • The evidence travels with the idea. The post carries the numbers that qualified the company, so your audience is not asked to take a shortlist on trust.
  • Each format names what it does NOT check. A turnaround format does not check valuation; an insider format does not check the business. Saying so is what keeps an idea from reading as a recommendation.
  • One idea per format per week is reserved for you, so two partners never publish the same company in the same week.

How Measurement Works ​

Every generated post receives a short tracked link. The link itself is opaque — it does not encode partner code, month, format, workflow, or ticker. Those values are stored as reporting fields instead.
