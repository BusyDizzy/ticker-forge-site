What We Give A Partner ​

Partner Requirements states what we ask of a partner. This page states the other half: what TickerForge commits to giving in return, in the same plain terms.

Nothing here is conditional on results. Everything below starts the day a pilot is approved and runs for the whole pilot window.

TickerForge is educational decision-support software. It is not financial advice, a signal channel, a broker, or a promise of investment results.

⭐ Standard Access For The Whole Pilot ​

An approved pilot switches your own TickerForge account to Standard for the length of the pilot window. That is what makes partner posts worth publishing: they are built from the full research layer, not from the free public preview.

  • Screeners without the free ceiling. The TickerForge score range opens up instead of stopping at the public cut-off.
  • Higher Smart Alerts and tracking limits. More tracked companies, more alert subscriptions.
  • Standard-level portfolio reports and diagnostics.
  • Workspace on the website shows Partner pilot instead of Free, so the whole product behaves consistently across bot and site.

Access is granted by approval, not by payment. It is not a purchase, it does not make you a paying subscriber in our reports, and it does not consume an Early Access slot.

🧠 Content You Can Publish The Same Day ​

Every partner direction has a list of formats that are live in the Partner Cabinet today, and a list of formats available on request for a pilot. The three program pages carry the current lists:

ProgramStock Analysis & Ideas

Company research: Stock Verdict, Insider Activity, Fund Moves, and the weekly rubric ideas.

ProgramMarket News

Recurring market and macro context: Market Regime, its state-change alert, and U.S. Economic Health.

ProgramPortfolio Health

Portfolio diagnostics content, scoped per pilot rather than generated with one tap.

What we commit to about that content:

  • Real data, generated on demand. A post is the live product screen at the moment you press the button, not a template with your ticker dropped into it.
  • The same post the channel publishes. Formatting and the channel image included, so it can be forwarded as it arrives.
  • Honest refusals. When the underlying research is not there, the Cabinet says so and generates nothing. The state-change alert, for one, only exists on the weeks the market regime actually turned — you will never be handed a post announcing a change that did not happen.
  • One rubric idea per week is reserved for you, so two partners are not handed the same company on the same week.

🌐 Posts In Your Audience's Language ​

The Partner Cabinet has an English / Russian switch under Generate Post. It changes the whole post, not just the button under it, and it applies to every format. Your Cabinet stays in English either way — the setting is about what your readers get.

🔗 Tracked Links And Attribution ​

  • Every generated post carries your own tracked link, already inside the post's call to action. There is nothing to paste, and nothing to configure.
  • The link opens the bot, not a redirect page. A reader lands directly on the matching TickerForge workflow — the stock card, the market dashboard, the US Economy menu.
  • A personal referral code and link are issued automatically when your pilot is approved. That is a second, separate link for a bio, a story, or a comment — generated posts attribute readers on their own, without it.
  • Attribution is first-touch. The reader who arrives through your post is bound to you, and stays bound.

💰 Referral Rewards ​

Readers attributed through your partner links join the existing TickerForge referral program and earn under exactly the same rules as /ambassador — the partner program does not invent a separate rate.

  • 20% of a referred user's subscription, every month, for as long as that subscription stays active. There is no cap on referrals or on monthly rewards.
  • Calculated per calendar month from the paid plans your referred users held during that month.
  • Charged in Telegram Stars. Plans are priced in Stars; the USD figures on the pricing page are references, not a second price list.
  • Visible before it is paid. My Monthly Earnings in the bot is a live estimate from current subscription state — a working number, not a settled invoice.
  • Payout details are agreed individually once you have your first paying referrals. Ask through the bot or contact us if you want the terms confirmed before you start.

Rewards depend on how many referred users hold an active paid plan in a given month. Nothing here is a forecast of what a channel will earn.

📊 The Numbers We Report Back ​

A pilot is only worth running if both sides can see whether it worked. Inside the Partner Cabinet:

  • Statistics — referred users, active paying referrals this month, estimated monthly payout, and a breakdown per post type.
  • Bot starts per post type — how many readers actually opened TickerForge from each kind of post, so you can see which format your audience responds to.
  • Monthly report — the current or previous month as an XLSX snapshot, generated on request.
  • My Progress — status, access window, generated posts and verified posts.

What Is Not Included ​

An approved pilot grants Standard access and nothing beyond it. Pro-level access, bulk API or raw data feeds, custom or white-label reports, co-branded landing pages, paid placements, and category exclusivity are separate commercial discussions — all of them easier to justify once a first pilot has real numbers behind it. The full list is on Partner Requirements.
