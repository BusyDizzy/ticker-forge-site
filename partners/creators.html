Use TickerForge In Your Investing Content ​

You do not need to turn your channel into a TickerForge channel. Use TickerForge as one research layer inside the content your audience already watches or reads.

Add structured market data, stock checks, insider and fund activity, business quality, market regime, sector rotation, Smart Alerts, and portfolio risk to your existing investing content. Show a real check, explain what it adds to your research, and keep your own judgment.

Start with 14 days of Standard Review Access, after application review. No publications required. If it fits, agree a Pilot with 1–2 test posts, supported by research blocks and tracked links. After a successful pilot, discuss Active Partner terms: for example 2–4 posts per month, referral, paid or co-branded content.

TickerForge is educational decision-support software. It is not financial advice, a signal channel, a broker, a trading robot, a copy-trading system, or a promise of returns.

Who This Is For ​

  • YouTube investing creators
  • Instagram and TikTok finance creators
  • LinkedIn finance writers
  • Newsletter authors and investing bloggers
  • Personal finance educators
  • Portfolio-focused creators
  • Communities discussing stocks and market context, including Telegram communities

We look for creators who explain evidence, uncertainty, and risk. Creators whose main product is a signal group, copy-trading, claims of certain investment outcomes, or paid “buy/sell alerts” are not a fit.

Content Angles You Can Use ​

Pick one angle that fits your usual content:

  • Stock deep dive: use TickerForge as one data layer alongside your other research.
  • “How I find stocks worth researching”: explain a screening workflow and what you check next.
  • Portfolio risk review: explore concentration, sector exposure, and stress scenarios.
  • Market regime or sector rotation segment: put a company or portfolio in broader context.
  • Smart Alert follow-up: explain what changed and why it matters.
  • Insider/fund activity evidence block: add ownership context and discuss its limits.
  • Neutral tool walkthrough or review: show the workflow and give your own assessment.
  • Data-backed carousel or Reel/TikTok script: build a short explanation around a real diagnostic.
  • LinkedIn post or newsletter section: add a research example to a topic you already cover.
  • Description, bio, or pinned-comment tracked link test: pair the link with a qualified content example so viewers know what they can check.

Partner Attribution ​

Approved creators receive a tracked partner link with a partner/referral code for attribution and UTM parameters for analytics. UTMs alone do not identify a referral owner.

For example, replace the placeholder code and campaign with the values agreed for your pilot:

https://ticker-forge.com/portfolio-diagnostics?ref=creator_code&utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=creator_slug&utm_content=portfolio_review

Visitors who sign up, start the bot, or become paying users through an approved creator link can be attributed when the tracked link is preserved through signup or bot start, under the existing Ambassador/referral rules.

The website keeps the first creator code in that browser and carries it into supported email/Workspace signup and tracked Telegram handoffs. An existing first-touch referrer is not replaced. Browser storage, link preservation, and a successful handoff matter; attribution is not guaranteed across browsers or devices.

Email/Workspace leads retain the creator attribution in CRM. Referral rewards use the existing Ambassador identity and eligibility rules when the visitor connects to an eligible Telegram user account. A website visit or an email signup alone does not establish a reward.

What You Get ​

  • Standard access for the creator pilot: start with 14 days of Review Access, with no publications required. If you continue to a Pilot, we agree its access dates separately.
  • A tracked partner link: use it on the website, in a description, bio, story, pinned comment, or newsletter.

“Creator pilot” describes the collaboration; access uses the existing Standard partner access. It is not a separate subscription tier.

What We Ask In Return ​

  • No publications during Review Access are required. If you continue, agree 1–2 qualified content tests for the Pilot.
  • Keep your own voice and opinion.
  • Use a real TickerForge check, screenshot, diagnostic, or workflow.
  • Include the tracked link where the platform allows it.
  • Use educational context and sponsored/affiliate or complimentary-access disclosures where required.
  • Avoid promises of returns, personalized advice, and “buy/sell signal” framing.

The same three-stage partner requirements apply to creators. We agree the content format and Pilot timing individually.

Pilot Flow ​
