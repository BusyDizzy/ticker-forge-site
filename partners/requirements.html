Partner Requirements ​

Who can become a TickerForge partner, what a partner commits to, and when partner access ends.

The partner program is an exchange, not a giveaway. This page states both sides of it in plain terms so nobody has to guess: what we give, what we ask, and what causes access to stop.

TickerForge is educational decision-support software. It is not financial advice, a signal channel, a broker, or a promise of investment results.

🎁 What A Partner Gets ​

The same benefits the Partner Program hub shows inside the Telegram bot. What we give a partner is the full version of this list — product access, content, tracking, reporting and how referral rewards are calculated.

  • ⭐ Standard access. TickerForge Standard for the whole pilot window, so posts are built from the full research layer instead of the free public preview.
  • 🧠 Ready-to-post research. Live Stock Verdict, Insider Activity, Fund Moves, and Market Regime content you can quote inside your own posts.
  • 🔗 Tracked links. Every partner post can send readers straight into the matching TickerForge workflow through a tracked Telegram deep link.
  • 💰 Referral earnings. Readers attributed through your partner links join the existing TickerForge referral program and earn under the same rules as /ambassador.
  • 📊 Measurable results. Generated posts, tracked clicks, referrals, and estimated earnings in your Partner Cabinet inside the bot.

Who Can Become A Partner ​

  • Audience size: from 1,000 real subscribers or followers on the main channel you plan to post in — Telegram channel, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn group or page, newsletter, Discord, or forum community.
  • Real audience: organic subscribers. Bought followers, engagement pods, and bot-inflated view counts do not qualify, and are grounds for ending a pilot at any point.
  • Topic fit: the channel is about investing, markets, personal finance, or company analysis. TickerForge content has to fit what your audience already reads.
  • Live channel: at least 3 months of history and a regular posting rhythm — roughly 2 or more of your own posts per month.
  • Language: English or Russian audiences are supported today. Other languages are considered case by case.
  • Telegram account: approval binds the partner seat to one Telegram account, so you need to be reachable in Telegram even if your channel lives elsewhere.
  • Compliance fit: the channel does not provide trading signals, promise returns, run copy-trading, or market itself with profit guarantees.

Smaller audiences. The 1,000 threshold is a default, not a wall. A 300-member closed investor club, a consultant with a real client base, or a niche newsletter with high open rates can be approved case by case. Tell us the engagement numbers, not only the subscriber count.

What A Partner Commits To ​

  • Posting volume: the standard pilot is 3 qualified posts inside a 30-day access window. Both numbers are set per partner at approval and are visible in your Partner Cabinet as Required posts.
  • Ongoing cadence: if the pilot continues, the usual rhythm is 2-4 qualified posts per month.
  • Only verified posts count. The Cabinet shows generated posts and verified posts separately. Generating a post in the bot is not the same as publishing it: only published posts that we can verify count toward the requirement.
  • Disclosure: if a post is sponsored, affiliate-supported, or materially helped by complimentary partner access, say so in a way that fits your channel.
  • Personal seat: the access seat belongs to the approved partner. It is not transferable, not a team account, and must not be shared with your audience or resold.
  • Data use: TickerForge output is for your own posts. Republishing it as a standalone feed, database, or competing product is not part of the program.
  • Wording: keep partner posts on the educational side — market context and evidence, not personal recommendations or return promises. The rules are the same ones TickerForge follows in its own content.

What Counts As A Qualified Post ​

A post counts when all four are true:

  • it contains a real TickerForge check — an actual evidence block, screenshot, or diagnostic you ran, not a paraphrase;
  • it keeps your own voice and analysis — a bare screenshot with no commentary is not a partner post;
  • it carries a clear educational disclaimer;
  • it links to a tracked bot or product link so the result can be measured.

When Partner Access Is Paused Or Removed ​

  • The window ends. The access period runs out and is not extended.
  • The cadence stops. The agreed number of verified posts is not published during the window, or the ongoing monthly rhythm stops.
  • Posts do not qualify. Publications skip the real check, the disclaimer, or the tracked link, so nothing can be verified or measured.
  • The seat is shared. Access is passed to another person, used as a team or client account, resold, or handed to the audience.
  • The audience is not what it looked like. Subscriber counts or engagement turn out to be bought or artificially inflated.
  • The framing breaks the rules. TickerForge is presented as a signal service, a profit guarantee, a trading robot, or personalized advice.
  • Product data is redistributed. Output is republished as a standalone feed, database, or competing product.
  • Spam or fraud. Mass unsolicited posting, fake traffic to tracked links, or self-clicking to inflate pilot metrics.
  • You want to stop. A partner can end a pilot at any time, with no penalty and no obligation to explain.

For anything except abuse, the normal path is a conversation first: we ask what happened, and pause only if the cadence genuinely stopped. A pause is reversible — a paused or expired partner can be reactivated later without reapplying from scratch.

What Happens To Your Referrals ​

Partner access and referral earnings are separate things, and losing one does not erase the other.

  • Readers already attributed to you stay yours. Referral ownership is first-touch and permanent: it is not reassigned when a pilot ends, and those referrals keep earning under the standard Ambassador rules.
  • New attribution stops. While partner access is not live, clicks on partner links no longer create new referral ownership.
  • First touch wins. A reader who already belongs to another ambassador is never re-attributed to a partner link.

What Is Not Included Automatically ​

An approved pilot grants Standard access and nothing beyond it. These are separate commercial discussions:

  • Pro-level access
  • bulk API or raw data feeds
  • custom or white-label reports
  • co-branded landing pages
  • paid placements and sponsorship budgets
  • exclusivity in a niche or language

All of these can be discussed after a first measured pilot, and some of them are easier to justify once real numbers exist.
