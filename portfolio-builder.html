Conservative Is the Demo Portfolio ​

The web widget runs Conservative Quality, the portfolio type available to FREE users. It is the clearest way to test the portfolio engine: stable quality companies, steadier fundamentals, lower available volatility, diversification rules, and a market-aware cash buffer. It is still a virtual model, not a recommendation or a brokerage portfolio.

How the Portfolio Builder Works ​

Click Build Portfolio to request a Conservative Quality portfolio and view the result directly on this page. The engine evaluates the latest eligible companies rather than returning a fixed model portfolio. Candidates must pass fundamental quality, business risk, liquidity, and portfolio construction rules. The result is therefore allowed to change as company data and market conditions change.

TickerForge shows the proposed companies, diversification context, Portfolio Health scores, and current cash allocation. The portfolio can retain more cash when the market is defensive or panicked. Read about these checks in the Portfolio Health Score methodology and the Market Stress Index.

What You Receive in Telegram ​

The first response is a portfolio blueprint, not a buy instruction. It shows the candidates that passed today, the proposed structure, and whether current conditions support investing the full virtual balance. If too few companies pass the rules, TickerForge can decline to build the portfolio instead of filling it with weaker names.

After reviewing the blueprint, you can create the portfolio inside Telegram and use it as an educational tracking account. The portfolio remains separate from your broker and uses virtual positions. TickerForge does not execute trades or move money.
