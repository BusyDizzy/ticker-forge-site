Join the Early Tester Program ​

TickerForge is in early access. We are inviting a small group of users to test the portfolio analytics, watchlist monitoring, market regime, stock research, and portfolio diagnostics workflows before the wider launch.

Decision-support software, not financial advice.

TickerForge provides software-based stock research, portfolio diagnostics, market context, and decision-support workflows for educational and informational purposes. It does not provide personalized financial advice, brokerage services, trade execution, portfolio management, or guaranteed investment outcomes.

Why Join? ​

Early testers help us improve the product experience:

  • stock research workflow clarity;
  • portfolio diagnostics and stress-test usability;
  • market regime and sector context;
  • email and Telegram research notifications;
  • pricing, onboarding, and paid-plan messaging.

Early access is about product feedback. It is not a managed investment service, trading group, or brokerage workflow.

What You Get ​

  • Access to the TickerForge Telegram bot and browser widgets.
  • Early access to selected Standard/Pro software features while testing.
  • A private feedback path for bugs, workflow confusion, and feature ideas.
  • Optional founder/product feedback session.
  • Early-access pricing eligibility when paid subscriptions become available.

What We Ask ​

We ask testers to use the product honestly and tell us where the workflow is confusing, slow, or not useful.

Useful feedback includes:

  • which reports helped you understand a company or portfolio;
  • which screens were unclear;
  • which monitoring alerts you would actually use;
  • which paid software features would be worth upgrading for.

Ready? ​

  • Join the Telegram Bot
  • Request access via form

After the form, open the bot and use /earlyaccess if instructed by the onboarding flow.
