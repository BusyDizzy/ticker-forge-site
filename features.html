Features ​

TickerForge is an investing decision-support platform. It helps users organize public market data, analyze stocks, monitor watchlists, review portfolio health, track market regime context, and receive research notifications when selected indicators change.

Decision-support software, not financial advice.

TickerForge provides software-based stock research, portfolio diagnostics, market context, and decision-support workflows for educational and informational purposes. It does not provide personalized financial advice, brokerage services, trade execution, portfolio management, or guaranteed investment outcomes.

Systems-engineering mindset

TickerForge applies systems-engineering thinking to investing: monitor the inputs, diagnose weak points, stress test the structure, and review what changed before acting.

Proof you can inspect: The product exposes sample reports, public model portfolio diagnostics, stock checks, market regime context, and methodology pages so users can review how the software works. Portfolio Diagnostics demoStock Check widgetMarket WidgetMethodology hub

What this does not do: TickerForge is not a brokerage, investment adviser, managed portfolio service, or trade execution system. It is educational decision-support software.

Research Candidates ​

Use TickerForge to surface companies worth a closer look:

  • screen 3,000+ active stocks using fundamentals, valuation context, and quality indicators;
  • compare business quality, balance-sheet strength, and risk;
  • review Smart Money and insider activity reported through public filings;
  • track names where monitored indicators change.

TickerForge is not built to provide trade instructions. It is built to help you decide what deserves deeper research.

Review Stock Context ​

The stock workflow combines:

  • business quality;
  • valuation context;
  • financial resilience;
  • technical setup;
  • reported fund and insider activity;
  • market and portfolio context.

Use the Stock Check Widget to run a company-level research check.

Monitor Market Conditions ​

Markets change under the surface. TickerForge helps you review:

  • risk-on / risk-off context;
  • market stress;
  • sector rotation;
  • volatility and macro pressure;
  • daily and weekly market regime views.

Open the Market Regime Widget to compare the latest market views in the browser. For the plain-language framework behind those labels, read Market Regime Indicators Explained.

Diagnose Portfolio Health ​

TickerForge portfolio tools help users inspect:

  • sector concentration;
  • top-name concentration;
  • cash and exposure;
  • market beta sensitivity;
  • scenario stress-test estimates;
  • portfolio health and structural risk.

Start with Portfolio Diagnostics Demo or Import Portfolio.

Use Research Alerts ​

TickerForge alerts are monitoring notifications. They can notify you when selected watchlist, portfolio, market-risk, or indicator conditions change.

Alerts are not instructions to buy or sell. They are designed to bring research changes back to your attention.
