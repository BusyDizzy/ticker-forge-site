Market Regime ​

Five named conditions — Panic, Defensive, Recovery, Trend, Euphoria. One of them is true right now, and knowing which one changes how everything else should be read.

The Question It Answers ​

"Is this drop noise, or is it confirmation?"

The same 1% decline means opposite things in different conditions. Inside a Trend regime it is a pullback in a market with broad participation. Inside a Defensive one it is the market doing what a fragile market does. No headline can make that distinction, because a headline is about the 1%, not about the conditions around it.

Why A State Read Beats A News Feed ​

  • It is a series, not a stream. The regime is one label that persists for weeks. A reader can follow "Defensive → Recovery → Trend" across months and see the shape of a cycle. A news feed hands them a different topic every morning and no thread between them.
  • It changes rarely, and that is the point. Most days the answer is "the same as yesterday". A read that changes constantly is not measuring a state, it is measuring noise.
  • The change is announced separately. Both the daily snapshot and the weekly post always state the current regime, so a regime that just turned looks identical to one that has held for three weeks. A separate alert fires only on the days it actually changed — see the example below.
  • It is recorded. Every regime read is stored with its date. Nobody has to take a channel's word for what it said last month.
  • The rule is public. Cross-asset trend panel, macro score, ADX cross-validation — the method is written down and a reader can disagree with it on the merits.

How It Is Built ​

A cross-asset trend panel across equities, credit, rates, the dollar and volatility produces a macro score; the score maps to one of the five regimes and is cross-validated against trend strength so a single wobbling input cannot flip the label on its own.
